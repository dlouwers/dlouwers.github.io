# Cover prompts

These produced covers that match the existing ones (isometric, dark, glowing
traces). Replace the scene paragraphs; keep the style lines and the last line.

```
Isometric flat vector illustration, 16:9 landscape, at least 1536 px wide,
clean outlined shapes with soft gradients and gentle glow, in the style of a
modern tech blog cover. Dark slate-navy background with a faint isometric grid.
Glowing circuit-board traces with small round nodes run across the floor in
teal, warm orange and coral.

<Left: …>
<Centre: …>
<Right: …>

Mood: calm, precise, trustworthy. Muted pastel teal and grey objects, accents
in orange and coral. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people.
```

Lay the scene out left to right; a scene described top to bottom comes back
as a square or portrait image.

To fix an image that came back square:

```
Extend this image to a 16:9 landscape canvas by adding space on the left and
right. Keep every existing element exactly where it is, at the same size, and
do not crop the top or bottom. Continue the dark slate-navy background, the
faint isometric grid and the teal, orange and coral circuit traces naturally
into the new areas. Do not add new objects, text, letters or logos.
```

If the generator draws something that looks like a logo (a ship's wheel is a
common one), follow up with "remove all text and symbols".
